Creator Uploader

A private publishing tool for the Jack of All Clips and Anime Easter Eggs channels.

Creator Uploader is a small program that runs on its owner's own computer. It takes a finished short video and publishes it to the owner's own YouTube channels, Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages, after the owner has reviewed and approved the title, description and settings for each post.

It is not offered to the public. It has no sign-up, no user accounts and no service of its own.
