Privacy Policy

Creator Uploader · last updated 28 October 2026

What Creator Uploader is

Creator Uploader is a private, single-user program that runs on its owner's own computer. Its only job is to upload videos that the owner made to the owner's own YouTube channels, Instagram accounts, Facebook Pages and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

What data it accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, the program uploads videos, sets thumbnails, playlists and video details, and reads those same videos back to confirm the upload, all on channels owned by the operator. Through the Meta Graph API it publishes videos to the operator's own Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages and reads those posts back. Through the TikTok Content Posting API it publishes videos to the operator's own TikTok accounts and reads back the operator's own profile name and the posted video's details. It does not read, collect or store any information about viewers, subscribers, followers, commenters or any other person.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens issued by Google, Meta and TikTok are stored only in a local configuration file on the operator's computer. They are never uploaded, shared, sold or sent to any third party, and they are not shown in logs. Local logs record what was published (post IDs, titles and times) and nothing else.

Google API Services

Creator Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to publish and verify the operator's own videos and is never used for advertising, transferred to others or used to train models.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page and from the Facebook account's Business Integrations settings, and from TikTok's Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Deleting the local configuration file removes every stored token.
